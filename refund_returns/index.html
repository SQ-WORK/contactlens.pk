Refund and Returns Policy

Last Updated: October 2025

At ContactLens.pk, customer satisfaction is our top priority. We strive to ensure that every order reaches you in perfect condition and matches your expectations.
This Refund and Returns Policy explains when and how customers can request a return, replacement, or refund for their purchased products.

1. Eligibility for Returns

To maintain hygiene and product safety, only certain items are eligible for return or replacement.
Please read the following carefully before requesting a return:

Note: Contact lenses are delicate, hygienic medical items - once opened or handled, they cannot be accepted back for resale or reuse.

2. Return Request Process

If you receive a defective, wrong, or damaged product, please follow these steps:

  1. Contact our customer support within 48 hours of delivery.
  2. Provide order number, product name, and reason for return.
  3. Attach clear pictures of the product and packaging for verification.
  4. Our team will review your request and confirm the return process.

Once approved, we’ll guide you through the replacement or refund steps.

3. Refund Policy

Refunds are issued in the following cases:

  • Product is out of stock after payment
  • Wrong item or damaged item confirmed by our team
  • Order cancelled before dispatch

Refund Methods

Refunds may be processed through:

Refunds will only be made after product verification and approval by our quality control team.

4. Order Cancellation

You can cancel your order before it is dispatched.
Once shipped, cancellations are not possible, but you may still contact our support team for assistance.

How to Cancel:

  • Email or WhatsApp our support with your Order Number and Reason for Cancellation.
  • If approved, your payment will be refunded as per our policy above.

5. Exchange Policy

If you received the wrong color, power, or brand, we’ll gladly exchange it (if unused and sealed).
Replacement shipping charges may apply in some cases.

We recommend checking your prescription details carefully before confirming your order to avoid mismatch issues.

6. Damaged or Missing Items

If your package arrives damaged or an item is missing:

  • Report the issue within 24–48 hours of delivery.
  • Keep the original packaging and product for inspection.
  • Provide clear unboxing images or videos to verify the issue.

Once confirmed, we’ll send a replacement or refund according to the situation.

7. Important Conditions

Please note:

  • Products must be unused, sealed, and in original packaging.
  • Shipping fees are non-refundable unless the error is on our part.
  • Any unauthorized returns will not be accepted.
  • Refunds may take longer in case of courier delays or incomplete information.

8. Contact Us

For all return or refund-related inquiries, please reach out via the Contact Us page on our website.
Our support team is available during business hours to assist you promptly.
