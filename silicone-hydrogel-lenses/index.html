Why Silicone Hydrogel?

Standard hydrogel contact lenses (like Acuvue 2, Biomedics 55, Eye Soft) rely on water content to deliver oxygen to your cornea. The more water, the more oxygen. But also the more dryness in air-conditioned environments. Silicone hydrogel lenses use silicone-infused polymer chains that transmit oxygen directly through the lens material, independent of water content. The practical benefits for Pakistani wearers:

  • 5-6x more oxygen reaches your cornea: keeps eyes whiter and less red after 10+ hour wear
  • Less dry-eye discomfort in AC offices: Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad office workers report dramatically less afternoon dryness
  • Approved for extended/overnight wear on select brands (Acuvue Oasys up to 7 days, Air Optix Night & Day)
  • Better for first-time lens wearers: easier transition without the dryness shock
  • Safer for sensitive eyes: lower risk of corneal hypoxia (oxygen starvation)

Who Should Choose Silicone Hydrogel?

Switch to silicone hydrogel if any of these apply:

  • You work 8+ hours in an air-conditioned office and your eyes feel dry by afternoon
  • You want to wear lenses overnight (Acuvue Oasys is approved for this)
  • You have sensitive eyes or have experienced redness with standard hydrogel
  • You wear lenses 12+ hours per day (commuters, students, healthcare workers)
  • You're a first-time lens wearer wanting the easiest comfort transition

If you only wear lenses occasionally (events, weekends) or under 6 hours per day, standard hydrogel transparent lenses like Acuvue 2 or Biomedics 55 may be enough at a lower cost.

Care & Maintenance

Silicone hydrogel lenses require slightly different care than standard hydrogel. Use a multi-purpose solution specifically labeled for silicone hydrogel. Bausch & Lomb Biotrue and Alcon Opti-Free PureMoist are widely available in Pakistan. Avoid hydrogen peroxide-based solutions unless explicitly approved by your lens manufacturer. Replace your lens case every 3 months and never top up solution. Always discard and use fresh.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of silicone hydrogel contact lenses in Pakistan?
Silicone hydrogel contact lens prices in Pakistan range from PKR 5,900 for O2 Optix and Air Optix Colors up to PKR 16,950 for Bausch & Lomb Ultra. Acuvue Oasys sits at PKR 10,200. While more expensive than standard hydrogel lenses, the higher cost is offset by longer comfortable wear and reduced dry-eye risk for daily wearers.
Can I sleep in silicone hydrogel contact lenses?
Only specific silicone hydrogel lenses are approved for overnight wear. Acuvue Oasys is approved for up to 6 nights / 7 days continuous wear including sleep. Bausch & Lomb Ultra and O2 Optix are approved for daily wear only. Remove before sleeping. Air Optix Night & Day (if available) is approved for 30 nights continuous wear. Always check the specific approval on your lens packaging.
Are silicone hydrogel lenses better than standard hydrogel?
For most full-time wearers in Pakistan's hot, dry, often air-conditioned environments, yes. Silicone hydrogel lenses transmit 5-6x more oxygen, stay comfortable longer, and reduce afternoon dryness in offices. For occasional wear under 6 hours, standard hydrogel lenses like Acuvue 2 work fine at lower cost. The upgrade is most worthwhile for 10+ hour daily wearers and dry-eye sufferers.
Do silicone hydrogel lenses come in prescription power?
Yes. Acuvue Oasys, Bausch & Lomb Ultra, and O2 Optix are all available in prescription powers from -0.50 to -10.00 Sph in 0.25 increments. Plus and Toric (astigmatism) versions are available in some brands. WhatsApp your prescription card to +92 321 9455233 and we'll confirm power availability for your chosen brand.
What lens solution should I use with silicone hydrogel?
Use a multi-purpose solution specifically formulated for silicone hydrogel lenses. Bausch & Lomb Biotrue and Alcon Opti-Free PureMoist are widely available in Pakistan and approved for all major silicone hydrogel brands. Avoid generic or expired solutions, and never use water (tap or bottled) to rinse lenses. Only sterile lens solution.
