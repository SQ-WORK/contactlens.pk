What "Hidrocor Style" Means

Hidrocor pigment technology refers to a specific colored-lens design where the iris pigment is solid (not patterned), there's no defined dark outer ring (limbal ring), and the color blends softly into your natural iris edge. This produces the most natural-looking colored lens effect. The eye still reads as "your eye" but in a different color, rather than looking like you're wearing a noticeable colored disc.

This style works particularly well on Pakistani dark brown eyes because the pigment is opaque enough to cover the natural brown color without the artificial-looking dark ring that some other colored lenses use to compensate for low opacity.

How Hidrocor-Style Compares to Other Colored Lens Styles

  • Versus Bella Diamond (three-tone realism): Hidrocor is single-tone solid pigment, Bella Diamond uses three pigment layers to mimic iris depth. Hidrocor looks softer and more subtle; Diamond looks more depthful. Both flatter Pakistani eyes.
  • Versus Bella Highlight (dark limbal ring): Hidrocor has no limbal ring, Highlight has a strong defined ring. Hidrocor looks natural; Highlight creates the trendy enlarged-eye effect.
  • Versus Freshlook ColorBlends: Hidrocor uses solid pigment, Freshlook uses 3-in-1 color blends. Both are subtle. Hidrocor is more realistic; ColorBlends has more pigment variation per lens.
  • Versus Bella Elite (high-opacity): Hidrocor is medium opacity, Elite is high opacity. Hidrocor lets some natural iris show through; Elite covers it completely.

Best Hidrocor Shades for Pakistani Skin Tones

Pakistani complexions cover a wide range. What works varies significantly:

  1. Wheatish skin (most common Pakistani skin tone): Hidrocor Mocca, Hidrocor Gray, and Hidrocor Topaz all flatter wheatish skin. Mocca is the most natural daily wear.
  2. Fair skin: Aquamarine Eldorado, Eldorado Tiffany, and Hidrocor Topaz work beautifully with fair Pakistani skin tones. Greys appear more pronounced on fair skin.
  3. Deep / dark skin: Hidrocor Mocca and Hidrocor Gray work. Deeper shades that don't appear too pale against rich skin tones. Avoid the lighter blue/aquamarine shades which can look artificial against deep skin.

Care Instructions

Hidrocor-style lenses use water content typical of soft hydrogel material. Clean with multi-purpose solution after each wear, replace the case every 3 months, and discard the lenses according to the replacement schedule on the package. Don't wear overnight (these are daily-wear lenses, not extended-wear). Maximum daily wear time is 8-10 hours for most wearers.
