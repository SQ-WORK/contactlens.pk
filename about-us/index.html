About ContactLens.pk

ContactLens.pk is Pakistan's trusted online contact lens store, supplying original branded lenses from authorized international and Korean distributors to customers across all 500+ Pakistani cities.

We started ContactLens.pk because finding genuine contact lenses in Pakistan was harder than it should be. Optical shops carry limited shades, prices vary wildly between sellers, and informal markets are flooded with counterfeit Bella and Dahab lenses that risk corneal damage. We built one place where Pakistani buyers can find the full range of original lenses at consistent prices, with transparent dispatch and cash-on-delivery payment.

What We Carry

Our catalog covers every major contact lens category sold in Pakistan:

  • Colored contact lenses from Bella, Dahab, Freshlook, Glimpse, Hydro, Adore, Eye Soft, and more. 46+ shades across daily, monthly, and yearly wear schedules.
  • Prescription lenses for weak eyesight from Acuvue, Bausch & Lomb, Biomedics, Hoya, Eye Soft. Covering powers from -0.50 to -10.00 Sph.
  • Silicone hydrogel lenses for maximum oxygen and all-day comfort. Acuvue Oasys, Bausch & Lomb Ultra, O2 Optix, Air Optix Colors.
  • Toric lenses for astigmatism. Biomedics Toric, Acuity Toric, Dahab Toric Eyesight.
  • Lens solutions and accessories: multi-purpose solutions, lens cases, eye drops.

Our Commitments

  • 100% original products. Every lens is sourced through authorized brand distribution. We do not sell loose-pack, unbranded, or counterfeit lenses regardless of profit margin. Our suppliers include direct distribution partners for Bella (Egypt), Dahab (Kuwait/Korea), and authorized Pakistani distributors for J&J, Bausch & Lomb, and CooperVision.
  • Transparent pricing. Prices on our website are the prices you pay. No hidden delivery surprises, no commission games. Rs 350 flat delivery nationwide.
  • Honest stock information. If a shade is out of stock or your prescription power isn't available, we tell you before taking your order. We don't oversell or substitute without your approval.
  • Customer-first WhatsApp support. Real humans answering at +92 321 9455233. For product questions, prescription verification, sizing advice, or post-delivery support.

Who We Serve

Our customers include:

  • Everyday colored lens wearers: university students, office professionals, and homemakers wanting natural enhancement
  • Brides and event-goers: Pakistani wedding bookings for coordinated bridal lens looks
  • People with weak eyesight: switching from glasses to convenient daily-wear contact lenses
  • Optical shops and salons: wholesale reseller pricing on bulk orders
  • Makeup artists and photographers: colored lens rentals and event-day shade packs

Where We Are

We deliver across all major Pakistani cities including Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Faisalabad, Multan, Quetta, Hyderabad, Gujranwala, Sialkot, Bahawalpur, Sargodha, and 500+ smaller cities and towns.
