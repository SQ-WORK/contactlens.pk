Eye Lenses for Weak Eyesight

If you've been prescribed glasses by your optometrist, you can switch to contact lenses for the same prescription. Reading your prescription card:

  • SPH (Sphere): your main power. Negative numbers (-0.50, -1.00, -2.00) are for myopia (nearsightedness). Positive numbers (+0.50, +1.00) are for hyperopia (farsightedness). Most Pakistani buyers have negative SPH.
  • CYL (Cylinder): astigmatism correction. If your prescription has CYL value (-0.75, -1.25, etc.), you need a Toric lens like Biomedics Toric or Acuity Toric. Not a regular spherical lens.
  • AXIS: direction of astigmatism. Only relevant if you need Toric lenses.
  • BC (Base Curve): usually 8.4mm, 8.5mm, or 8.6mm. Most contact lenses in our range are 8.6mm which fits the majority of Pakistani eyes.
  • DIA (Diameter): usually 14.0mm to 14.5mm. Standard.

Daily vs Monthly Transparent Lenses

Daily disposables (Bella One Day Transparent, Focus Dailies, Clear Vision Daily, Freshkon One-Day) are worn once and discarded. Best for occasional wear, travel, or anyone with hygiene-sensitive eyes. Higher cost per wear but zero cleaning required.

Monthly disposables (Acuvue 2, Biomedics 55, Eye Soft, Bausch & Lomb Soflens 59) are reused for 30 days with daily cleaning in multi-purpose solution. Best balance of cost and convenience for most Pakistani full-time wearers.

Bi-weekly / weekly extended wear (Acuvue Oasys) is approved for up to 7 days continuous wear including overnight. Convenient but requires high oxygen permeability. Acuvue Oasys uses silicone hydrogel for this reason.

Silicone Hydrogel vs Standard Hydrogel

Newer silicone hydrogel transparent lenses (Acuvue Oasys, Bausch & Lomb Ultra, O2 Optix, Air Optix) allow 5-6x more oxygen to reach the cornea compared to standard hydrogel. This means whiter, healthier-looking eyes after long wear and lower risk of dry-eye discomfort. The trade-off is higher price. Silicone hydrogel lenses cost PKR 9,000+ vs PKR 3,000-4,000 for standard hydrogel.

Water Content & Comfort

Water content affects how comfortable a lens feels on dry eyes. Higher water = more comfortable initially but can dry out faster in air-conditioned offices:

  • Acuvue 2: standard water
  • Biomedics 55: standard water
  • Bausch & Lomb Ultra: standard water (silicone hydrogel. Feels comfortable despite lower water)
  • Hoya: standard water (suits warm dry climates)

If you work in air-conditioned offices in Karachi, Lahore, or Islamabad and suffer from dry-eye discomfort, ask us on WhatsApp about silicone hydrogel options.
