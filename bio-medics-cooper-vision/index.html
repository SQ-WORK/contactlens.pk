About CooperVision and the Biomedics Range

CooperVision is one of the world's three largest contact lens manufacturers, alongside Johnson & Johnson (Acuvue) and Alcon (Dailies, Air Optix). Founded in 1980 and headquartered in California, CooperVision operates manufacturing facilities in the United States and United Kingdom, supplying contact lenses to optometrists and patients in over 100 countries.

The Biomedics product line is CooperVision's mid-range monthly disposable lens family. Designed for everyday wearers who want reliable manufacturing quality and clinical safety standards without paying premium-brand pricing. In Pakistan, Biomedics has been a trusted optometrist-recommended brand for over a decade.

What Makes CooperVision Biomedics Different

  • UK and US manufacturing: Biomedics lenses are produced in CooperVision facilities under FDA (US) and CE (European) regulatory oversight. This contrasts with many lower-cost colored lenses on the Pakistani market manufactured under less stringent standards.
  • FDA approved: Biomedics 55 and Biomedics Toric are both FDA-cleared for vision correction in the United States.
  • Predictable optics: CooperVision's manufacturing tolerances are tight, which means each pair of Biomedics lenses delivers consistent visual sharpness across the full 30-day wear cycle.
  • UV-blocking: Biomedics 55 blocks both UVA and UVB radiation, providing additional eye protection on top of vision correction (though UV-blocking contact lenses are not a substitute for sunglasses).

  • Biomedics Toric: toric monthly disposable lens for astigmatism correction. Requires a prescription with SPH (sphere), CYL (cylinder), and AXIS values. PKR 5,950 per box of 6 lenses.

CooperVision vs Acuvue vs Alcon

The three largest global contact lens manufacturers each have different brand positioning:

  • Johnson & Johnson (Acuvue): premium pricing, heavy marketing, oxygen-permeable silicone hydrogel emphasis. Acuvue Oasys is FDA-approved for 6-night extended wear.
  • Alcon (Dailies, Air Optix): daily disposable and extended-wear focus. Air Optix Night & Day is approved for 30-night extended wear (the highest available).
  • CooperVision (Biomedics): mid-range pricing for reliable monthly disposable quality. Less marketing spend, similar safety profile.

For monthly disposable transparent prescription lenses, CooperVision Biomedics offers the best balance of quality and price for Pakistani buyers. Typically 30-40% cheaper than the equivalent Acuvue 2 or Air Optix Aqua at comparable clinical safety.
