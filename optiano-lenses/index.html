About Optiano

Optiano (optiano.com) is a contact lens brand offering colored and transparent lenses for everyday wear, both with and without eyesight correction. ContactLens.pk is bringing the Optiano range to Pakistani customers, starting with the Optiano Toric astigmatism lens.

Optiano Toric. PKR 6,200

Optiano Toric is an astigmatism-correcting soft contact lens built on a hydrophilic lens material for accuracy in every critical parameter. It offers enhanced visual acuity and a precise fit, and has been shown to combat late-day dryness in contact lens wearers.

  • Base curve: 8.6mm
  • Diameter: 14.2mm
  • Water content: 55%
  • Replacement cycle: Monthly
