Biomedics Spherical and Toric Contact Lenses

Two Biomedics products are imported into Pakistan, each serving a different prescription type:

  • Biomedics 55: spherical monthly disposable. For correcting standard myopia (minus power, nearsightedness) and hyperopia (plus power, farsightedness). PKR 3,450 per box (6 lenses, 3-month supply).
  • Biomedics Toric: toric monthly disposable. For correcting astigmatism in addition to myopia or hyperopia. Requires a prescription with SPH + CYL + AXIS values. PKR 5,950 per box (6 lenses, 3-month supply).

Material and Comfort Specifications

Both Biomedics products use hydrogel material with soft hydrogel material. A long-established balance between comfort, optical quality, and oxygen transmission. This is the same water-content category as most other quality monthly disposable lenses available in Pakistan.

What you can expect from Biomedics 55 hydrogel comfort:

  • All-day wear tolerance: most wearers comfortably wear Biomedics for 8-12 hours daily without dry eye symptoms (provided the prescription is correct and the lens fit is verified by an optometrist).
  • Reduced dryness in air-conditioning: the soft hydrogel material holds moisture better than older 38%-water lenses. This matters for Pakistani office wearers in air-conditioned environments.
  • Stable optics across the 30-day cycle: the lens maintains shape and prescription accuracy from day 1 through day 30 when properly cleaned and stored.
  • UV-blocking: Biomedics 55 blocks UVA and UVB radiation. Additional eye protection during daytime outdoor exposure (though sunglasses are still recommended for direct sun).

Who Biomedics Is Best For

  • Daily contact lens wearers who want reliable monthly disposable quality without paying premium-brand pricing.
  • Office workers in air-conditioned environments where eye dryness matters. Biomedics 55's water content helps reduce dry-eye symptoms.
  • Astigmatism patients (those with SPH, CYL, and AXIS in their prescription). Biomedics Toric is one of the more affordable toric options in Pakistan compared to Acuvue Astigmatism or Air Optix Toric.
  • Buyers transitioning from Acuvue or Air Optix who want similar clinical safety at lower cost.
  • Optometrist-recommended buyers looking for an FDA-cleared, internationally distributed brand rather than unbranded or grey-market lenses.

Biomedics Care and Replacement Schedule

  1. Monthly replacement: Biomedics products are 30-day replacement lenses. Open a new pair on the first of each month and discard at month-end regardless of total wear hours.
  2. Daily cleaning: remove lenses each evening, rub with multi-purpose solution for 10-15 seconds per side, rinse, and store in fresh solution overnight. Don't top up old solution.
  3. Lens case: replace your storage case every 3 months. Old cases develop biofilm that contaminates fresh solution.
  4. Maximum daily wear: 12 hours for most wearers, less for those with dry-eye tendency. Don't sleep in Biomedics. These are daily-wear, not extended-wear lenses.
  5. Annual eye exam: recheck your prescription and corneal health yearly even if vision feels stable. Long-term contact lens wear can change corneal shape gradually.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many lenses are in a Biomedics box?
Both Biomedics 55 and Biomedics Toric come in boxes of 6 lenses, which provides a 3-month supply when worn as monthly disposables (one pair per month, replacing on the first of each month).
Is Biomedics 55 comfortable in Pakistani heat and humidity?
Yes. Biomedics 55 hydrogel material with soft hydrogel material handles Pakistani summer heat and humidity well for most wearers. The water content helps maintain moisture in dry indoor environments (air-conditioned offices) and the lens material doesn't shrink in heat. For maximum comfort during summer, also use preservative-free eye drops as needed and limit daily wear to 10-12 hours.
Can I sleep in Biomedics 55 or Biomedics Toric?
No. Biomedics products are daily-wear lenses only, not extended-wear. Sleeping in them significantly increases infection risk because the closed-eye environment reduces oxygen flow to the cornea. Always remove before sleeping, even for short naps. For overnight wear, only FDA-approved extended-wear lenses like Acuvue Oasys (6 nights) or Air Optix Night & Day (30 nights) are appropriate, and even those are recommended only short-term.
What is the price of Biomedics contact lenses in Pakistan?
Biomedics 55 is PKR 3,450 per box of 6 lenses (3-month supply). Biomedics Toric is PKR 5,950 per box. Both include nationwide cash on delivery and require prescription verification before order processing.
Do Biomedics lenses block UV light?
Biomedics 55 blocks both UVA and UVB radiation, providing additional eye protection during daytime outdoor exposure. However, UV-blocking contact lenses are not a substitute for sunglasses. They only cover the iris area, not the surrounding eye structures (eyelids, conjunctiva). Use sunglasses for direct sun exposure alongside the lens UV protection.
