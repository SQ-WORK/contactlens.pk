Last Updated: October 2025

We do not collect or store your card details on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Improve our website’s performance and usability
  • Communicate updates, offers, and order confirmations
  • Respond to inquiries and customer service requests
  • Ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations

We may also use anonymized data for analytics and marketing optimization.

3. Data Security

We implement strict security measures to protect your information, including:

  • SSL encryption for secure transactions
  • Firewall and malware protection
  • Limited data access for authorized personnel only

Although no online platform is 100% secure, we take every reasonable step to safeguard your data.

4. Cookies Policy

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience.
Cookies help us remember your preferences and analyze site traffic.

You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings.
However, some parts of the site may not function properly without them.

Types of Cookies Used:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for basic site functions
  • Performance Cookies: Help analyze site usage
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences
  • Advertising Cookies: Show relevant promotions and offers

We may share limited data with trusted third parties who help us operate our business, such as:

These partners are bound by confidentiality and data protection agreements.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal data
  • Ask for correction or deletion of your information
  • Withdraw consent to marketing communications
  • File a complaint if you believe your data is misused

To exercise these rights, please contact us via our website’s Contact Us page.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfill the purposes described in this policy

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 and above.

If we learn that information has been provided by a minor, we promptly delete it.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements.
