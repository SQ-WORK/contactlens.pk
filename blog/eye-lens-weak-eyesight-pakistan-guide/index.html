Eye Lens for Weak Eyesight in Pakistan: Complete Guide

Switching from glasses to contact lenses can change your daily life. Here's the complete Pakistani buyer's guide. How to read your prescription, what powers are available, which brand to pick, and current 2026 prices.

What Are "Weak Eyesight" Lenses?

"Weak eyesight" contact lenses (also called prescription lenses, power lenses, or vision-correcting lenses) correct the same refractive errors your glasses correct. Three main eyesight problems and the lens type each needs:

  • Myopia (nearsightedness): far objects are blurry. Corrected by negative-power lenses (-0.50, -1.00, -2.00 and so on, down to -10.00 or higher). Most Pakistani buyers have myopia.
  • Hyperopia (farsightedness): near objects are blurry. Corrected by positive-power lenses (+0.50, +1.00, +2.00).
  • Astigmatism: blurry vision at all distances due to irregular cornea shape. Corrected by Toric lenses requiring SPH + CYL + AXIS values.

How to Read Your Eye Prescription Card

Your optometrist provides a prescription card with values for each eye (OD = right, OS = left). Here's what each value means:

ValueWhat It Means
SPH (Sphere)Main power. Negative = myopia (-0.50 to -10.00). Positive = hyperopia (+0.50 to +5.00).
CYL (Cylinder)Astigmatism power. If non-zero, you need Toric lenses, not standard spherical.
AXISDirection of astigmatism (1° to 180°). Only relevant if CYL is non-zero.
BC (Base Curve)Lens curvature, usually 8.4mm, 8.5mm, or 8.6mm. Most contact lenses are 8.6mm.
DIA (Diameter)Lens size, typically 14.0mm to 14.5mm.
ADDReading addition power (for multifocal lenses only).

Important: Your glasses prescription is not identical to your contact lens prescription. Glasses sit ~12mm from your eye; contact lenses sit directly on the cornea. For powers above -4.00 Sph, the contact lens prescription is slightly different from the glasses prescription. Your optometrist will give you a specific contact lens prescription. Use that, not your glasses prescription.

Safety Tips for First-Time Eyesight Lens Wearers

  • Get a fitting first. Visit an optometrist to confirm your eye shape suits the lens base curve and diameter
  • Start with monthly disposables: easier than yearly, lower cost than daily disposables
  • Never sleep in standard hydrogel lenses: only Acuvue Oasys and silicone hydrogel lenses approved for overnight wear
  • Never use water on lenses: tap and bottled water both contain microbes
  • Replace your lens case every 3 months: biofilm builds up regardless of cleaning
  • Stop wearing immediately if eyes get red or sore: consult optometrist

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of weak eyesight contact lenses in Pakistan?
Weak eyesight contact lens prices in Pakistan range from PKR 1,700 for entry-level Sunsoft monthly disposables up to PKR 16,950 for premium Bausch & Lomb Ultra silicone hydrogel. Most popular mid-range options sit between PKR 3,150 (Hoya), PKR 3,450 (Biomedics 55), and PKR 11,950 (Acuvue 2). Colored lenses with prescription power start at PKR 5,400 (Bella Elite).
Which is the best contact lens for weak eyesight in Pakistan?
Best by use case: For affordable monthly daily wear, Biomedics 55 (PKR 3,450) or Bausch & Lomb Soflens 59 (PKR 3,300). For premium quality with UV-blocking, Acuvue 2 (PKR 11,950). For long AC office hours, silicone hydrogel Bausch & Lomb Ultra (PKR 16,950) or Acuvue Oasys (PKR 10,200). For dry eyes, silicone hydrogel options outperform standard hydrogel.

What power range do contact lenses come in?
Standard contact lenses come in powers from -0.50 to -10.00 Sph (myopia) and +0.50 to +5.00 Sph (hyperopia) in 0.25 increments. For very high myopia above -10.00, Glimpse Korean lenses offer extended powers up to -25.00 on select shades. For astigmatism, Toric lenses (Biomedics Toric, Acuity Toric) cover SPH + CYL + AXIS combinations.

How long should I wear contact lenses each day?
Most monthly disposable transparent lenses (Acuvue 2, Biomedics 55, Eye Soft) are approved for up to 12-14 hours daily wear.
