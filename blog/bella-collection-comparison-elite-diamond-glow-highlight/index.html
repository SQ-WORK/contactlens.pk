Bella Elite vs Diamond vs Glow vs Highlight: Pakistan Collection Guide

Bella has six different lens collections in Pakistan and most buyers can't tell them apart. Here's the clear breakdown. Elite vs Diamond vs Glow vs Highlight, with picks for your specific use case.

Bella is Pakistan's most popular colored contact lens brand, but the brand makes six different collections. And most buyers don't know which one suits them. Walk into any Lahore optical shop and ask for "Bella" and you'll get a follow-up: Elite? Diamond? Glow? Highlight? Natural? One Day? Each costs differently and looks different. Here's the clear breakdown.

Bella Elite. The Bestseller

Elite is Bella's flagship and the most-ordered colored lens in Pakistan. Single-tone high-opacity pigment with dense color coverage that fully transforms dark Pakistani brown eyes. 12+ shades in the range, 3-month replacement, PKR 5,400.

Elite shades available:

  • Emerald Green, Midnight Blue, Cloudy Grey, Amber Grey, Mint Grey, Lavender Grey
  • Grey Beige, Sandy Grey, Sandy Brown, Wild Honey, Silky Green, Crystal N

Choose Elite if:

  • You have dark Pakistani brown eyes and want dramatic visible transformation
  • You're getting married. Elite is the bridal favorite
  • You wear lenses primarily for events and special occasions
  • You want bold colors that show up in any lighting (especially flash)

Bella Diamond. Three-Tone Realism

If Elite is the dramatic transformation lens, Diamond is the believable everyday lens. Three-tone pigment technology layers a darker outer ring, a mid-tone band, and a lighter center to mimic the natural depth of a real iris. The result: at conversational distance and in close-up photos, Diamond looks more like real eye color than any other Bella collection.

Colored LensesBella Diamond 3 Month (90 Days)₨ 5,150View product

Diamond shades: Diamond Agate, Diamond Mysterious, Diamond Silver Mist, Diamond Oak. PKR 4,950 for 3-month wear.

Choose Diamond if:

  • You want colored lenses that look natural in close-up photos (interviews, dates, video calls)
  • You prefer realism over drama
  • You're okay with slightly less opacity than Elite in exchange for natural depth
  • You shoot a lot of close-up content (Instagram, professional photography)

Bella Glow. Radiant Subtle Enhancement

Glow is Bella's softest collection. Subtle radiant tones designed to enhance your natural eyes without obvious color change. The pigment is lower opacity than Elite or Diamond, so on dark eyes Glow reads as "brighter version of your existing eyes" rather than "different color".

Colored LensesBella Glow 3 Month (90 Days)₨ 4,300View product

PKR 4,500 for 3-month wear.

Choose Glow if:

  • You want colored lenses but don't want anyone to notice you're wearing them
  • You have light brown / hazel natural eyes (Glow works better on lighter eye bases)
  • You wear lenses to office or university and want maximum subtlety
  • You're new to colored lenses and want low-commitment enhancement

Bella Highlight. Defined Limbal Ring

Highlight is Bella's trendiest collection. The dolly-eye look with a strongly defined dark limbal ring around the colored iris. This creates the optical illusion of larger, more striking eyes. Most popular among Pakistani university students and content creators going for the "Korean idol" aesthetic.

Colored LensesBella Highlight 3 Month (90 Days)₨ 3,350View product

Highlight shades: Cool Hazel, Circle Gray, Pearl Gray. PKR 4,250 for 3-month wear.

Choose Highlight if:

  • You want the trendy dolly-eye / enlarging effect
  • You're younger (university age) and want the Instagram aesthetic
  • You're going to events where photos will be heavily filtered/edited
  • Your eyes are smaller and you want them to appear larger

Bella Natural. Translucent Daily Enhancement

Natural is Bella's most subtle collection. Translucent pigment with no limbal ring. The lens enhances iris pattern visibility rather than changing color. Best for buyers who want healthy-looking enhanced eyes without obvious color shift. Plano only (no prescription powers available).

PKR 3,950 for 3-month wear.

Choose Natural if:

  • You don't actually want colored eyes. You want brighter, healthier-looking eyes
  • You're a man buying lenses and don't want anyone to notice
  • You have prominent natural iris patterns you want to highlight
  • You don't need prescription power (Natural is plano only)

Bella One Day. Daily Disposable

The same Bella opacity in single-use daily disposable format. Wear once for 8-12 hours, then discard. No cleaning, no solution, no lens case. PKR 2,650 per pair.

All LensesBella One Day Hazel Honey (Daily Disposable)₨ 1,900View product

One Day shades: Almond Brown, Hazel Honey, Platinum Grey, Ash Brown, Rose Wood, Cedar, Pine.

Choose One Day if:

  • You only wear colored lenses occasionally (1-2 days per week)
  • You're a bride needing fresh lenses for each wedding event
  • You don't want to manage a daily cleaning routine
  • You have sensitive eyes and want maximum hygiene

What All Bella Collections Share

Despite the differences, all Bella collections share the same base specifications:

  • Material: soft hydrogel polymer (standard contact lens material)
  • Base curve: 8.6mm (standard fit for most Pakistani eyes)
  • Diameter: 14.2mm
  • Replacement schedule: 3-month (90-day) for most Bella collections; daily for Bella One Day
  • Prescription range: -0.50 to -8.00 Sph available on request for most collections

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Bella collection is best?
Best by use case: Bella Elite (PKR 5,400) for dramatic events and weddings. Highest opacity. Bella Diamond (PKR 4,950) for realistic close-up photos. Three-tone depth. Bella Glow (PKR 4,500) for subtle daily office wear. Bella Highlight (PKR 4,250) for trendy dolly-eye look. Bella Natural (PKR 3,950) for translucent enhancement. Bella One Day (PKR 2,650) for occasional wear with zero cleaning.
What's the difference between Bella Elite and Bella Diamond?
Bella Elite uses high-opacity single-tone pigment. Most dramatic transformation, best for evening events under flash. Bella Diamond uses three-tone layered pigment (darker outer ring, mid-band, light center). Most realistic in close-up photography. For barat/wedding day, Elite wins. For natural close-up portraits and video calls, Diamond looks more believable. Price difference is PKR 450.
Is Bella Glow good for dark Pakistani eyes?
Bella Glow has lower pigment opacity than Elite. It enhances rather than transforms. On dark Pakistani brown eyes, Glow reads as 'brighter version of your existing eyes' rather than a clearly different color. For dramatic transformation on dark eyes, choose Elite instead. Glow is best for buyers wanting subtle enhancement and for buyers with naturally lighter brown / hazel eyes.
Which Bella collection is best for first-time wearers?
For first-time colored lens wearers, Bella One Day Hazel Honey (PKR 2,650, daily disposable) is the safest start. No cleaning required, fresh pair every wear, low commitment if you decide colored lenses aren't for you. For reusable first-time wearers, Bella Natural or Glow are subtle entry points before stepping up to Elite or Diamond drama.
