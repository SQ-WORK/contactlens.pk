Bella vs Dahab vs Freshlook: Which Colored Lens Brand Should You Buy?

The three biggest colored lens brands in Pakistan have very different strengths. Here's an honest comparison across price, comfort, opacity, shade range, and prescription support. With our pick for each use case.

If you're buying colored lenses in Pakistan, you'll quickly narrow your choice down to three brands: Bella (Egypt), Dahab (Kuwait-designed, Korea-made), or Freshlook ColorBlends (Alcon, USA/Ireland). All three are widely available, all three have prescription support, and all three are FDA-equivalent certified.

But they're not interchangeable. Each has clear strengths and weaknesses. Here's the honest breakdown so you can pick the right one for your situation.

Bella. Best for Dramatic Transformation on Dark Pakistani Eyes

Bella's biggest strength is opacity. The Elite collection uses single-tone pigment dense enough to fully cover dark Pakistani brown irises. When you put in a Bella Elite Emerald Green or Midnight Blue, your eyes look genuinely green or blue, not a muddy mix of brown and pigment.

Bella also has the widest shade range. 40+ shades across six collections including specialty looks (the Glow line for radiant tones, Highlight for the dolly-eye limbal ring effect, Natural for translucent enhancement). If you want choice, Bella has more shades than Dahab and Freshlook combined.

Choose Bella if:

  • You have dark brown Pakistani eyes and want dramatic visible color transformation
  • You want to try many shades over time (start with Elite, expand into Diamond, Glow, Highlight)
  • You're budget-conscious. Bella One Day at PKR 2,650 is the cheapest premium colored lens
  • You want UV-blocking colored lenses

Skip Bella if:

  • You wear lenses 12+ hours in AC offices and need maximum hydration (Freshlook is better)
  • You want the most realistic close-up appearance (Dahab Platinum or Freshlook 3-in-1 are more believable up close)

Dahab. Best for Premium Wedding Photography

Dahab sits a tier above Bella in premium feel and Korean manufacturing quality. The pixel pattern across the iris is more refined, the limbal ring (where present) is more naturally graded, and the shade selection skews toward warmer Middle Eastern tones that flatter Pakistani skin particularly well.

Dahab Gold collection (PKR 3,150) is the sweet spot. 20 warm-toned shades, complimentary lens solution sachet + eye drops + branded case included in every box. The Platinum collection (PKR 3,500) adds premium pixel patterns for high-end photography use.

All LensesDahab Gold₨ 3,150View product

Where Dahab really wins: prescription power range. Available from -1.00 to -10.00 Sph in 0.50 increments. Wider than any other colored lens brand in Pakistan. For weak-eyesight buyers wanting colored lenses, Dahab is the only practical option above -8.00 Sph.

Choose Dahab if:

  • You're getting married. Dahab's warm tones and premium feel are popular for Pakistani weddings
  • You have high prescription power (above -8.00 Sph) and need colored lenses
  • You want premium presentation. Dahab's gold box and accessories feel like a luxury purchase
  • You're shopping for Middle Eastern aesthetic shades (Sun Kiss, Solitaire, Hind, Tiffany Blue)

Skip Dahab if:

  • You want maximum opacity on very dark eyes (Bella Elite has higher pigment density)
  • You need 55%+ water content for dry eyes (Freshlook is better hydrated)

Freshlook ColorBlends. Best for Long Office Wear & Natural Look

Freshlook ColorBlends has two killer advantages: soft hydrogel material (vs 38% for Bella and Dahab) and 3-in-1 color blend technology. The water content makes Freshlook the most comfortable colored lens for long daily wear in Pakistani offices. The 3-in-1 pigment layers three colors per lens. Yellow + brown + black for Pure Hazel, for example. Recreating the natural depth of a real iris.

The result: Freshlook ColorBlends looks more natural up close than either Bella or Dahab single-tone shades. It's the lens optometrists prescribe to first-time colored lens wearers and people moving from clear lenses to colored.

The downside: only 8 shades available in Pakistan (vs Bella's 40+ and Dahab's 30+). PKR 5,850 per box. Pricier than Bella Elite by PKR 450 per pair.

Choose Freshlook if:

  • You work in an AC office 8+ hours and your eyes go dry by afternoon
  • You want the most natural-looking colored lens for close-up settings (meetings, dating, photoshoots)
  • You wear contact lenses every day and need maximum comfort
  • You're a first-time colored lens buyer. Freshlook is the safest introduction

Skip Freshlook if:

  • You have very dark eyes and want maximum visible transformation (Bella Elite is more opaque)
  • You want shade variety. Only 8 Freshlook shades are available in Pakistan

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is better: Bella or Dahab?
Bella and Dahab serve slightly different needs. Bella has higher pigment opacity for dark eye transformation, wider shade range (40+ vs 30+), and is cheaper at the entry tier (PKR 2,650 One Day vs Dahab PKR 3,150 Gold). Dahab has more premium presentation, better Middle Eastern warm tones, and wider prescription range (up to -10.00 Sph vs Bella's -8.00). For everyday colored lenses, Bella wins. For premium wedding use and high prescriptions, Dahab wins.
Is Freshlook better than Bella for daily wear?
Yes, Freshlook ColorBlends is more comfortable for daily wear of 8+ hours due to its soft hydrogel material (vs Bella's 38%). This matters especially in air-conditioned Pakistani offices where dry-eye risk is higher. However, Bella has higher pigment opacity and shows up more dramatically on dark Pakistani brown eyes. For comfort, choose Freshlook. For drama, choose Bella.
Which colored lens brand has the most shades?
Bella has the most shades (40+) across six collections: Elite (12+ premium shades), Diamond (realistic three-tone), Glow (radiant tones), Highlight (limbal ring), Natural (translucent), and One Day (daily disposable). Dahab has 30+ shades across Gold (20), Platinum (10), and One Day. Freshlook ColorBlends has only 8 shades in Pakistan but they're considered the most natural-looking due to 3-in-1 color blend technology.
Which colored lens is best for first-time wearers?
For first-time colored lens wearers in Pakistan, Bella One Day Hazel Honey (PKR 2,650, daily disposable) is the safest choice. No cleaning required, fresh pair every wear, low infection risk. Freshlook ColorBlends Pure Hazel (PKR 5,850, monthly) is also excellent for first-timers due to its high water content and natural look. Both are forgiving for buyers still learning lens insertion technique.
