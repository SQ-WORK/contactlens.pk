Across mehndi, valima, reception, and bridal shoots, your eye color shows up in maybe 2,000-3,000 final images. The right shade enhances every photo. Daily vs Monthly vs Yearly. Which Schedule for Wedding Use?

If you only need lenses for the wedding events themselves (3-4 days), choose daily disposables. Bella One Day Hazel Honey (PKR 2,650 per pair) or Bella One Day Platinum Grey for grey events. Fresh pair every event, zero cleaning, lowest infection risk.

Wedding Day Practical Tips

  • Buy 1-2 spare pairs. Lenses get lost, dropped on hotel floors, accidentally thrown out with tissues. Spares prevent panic.
  • Insert lenses BEFORE eye makeup. Always. Eye makeup particles transfer to lenses if applied first.
  • Carry a lens case + solution to every event. Even for daily disposables. Sometimes you need to take a lens out mid-event.
  • Bring eye drops. Long days under heat and flash photography dry out eyes faster than expected. Multi-purpose lubricating eye drops are essential.
  • Don't sleep in lenses. Even after exhausting wedding days. Use daily disposables and discard, or remove monthly lenses before sleeping.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best contact lens for Pakistani brides?
The most-ordered bridal contact lens in Pakistan is Bella Elite Cloudy Grey. High-opacity cool silver-grey that photographs beautifully in flash and natural light. Other top picks: Dahab Hind (natural white-grey for close-up photos), Bella Elite Wild Honey (warm hazel-honey for gold-themed outfits), and Bella Diamond Silver Mist (realistic three-tone grey).
Should brides use daily or monthly contact lenses?
For wedding events only (3-4 days of use), daily disposable lenses like Bella One Day Hazel Honey or Bella One Day Platinum Grey are ideal. Fresh pair every day, zero cleaning, lowest infection risk. For continued use after the wedding, 3-month Bella Elite lenses offer lower cost per wear.
Which lens color photographs best for wedding photoshoots?
Grey lenses photograph best in flash and natural light. They don't compete with bridal makeup or outfit colors and create luminous depth in close-up shots. Top picks: Bella Elite Cloudy Grey, Dahab Hind (no limbal ring for natural look), and premium Glimpse Swarovski (crystal pixel pattern). For warm-toned outfits, hazel shades like Bella Elite Wild Honey or Glimpse Hazelnut work better.
How many pairs of bridal contact lenses should I order?
Order at least 2 pairs of your primary shade. One for the main event and one spare. For multi-event Pakistani weddings (mehndi, mayoon, barat, valima), consider 2-3 different shades matching each event's mood: warm hazel for mehndi, grey for evening barat, lighter hazel for valima.
