Daily vs Monthly vs 3-Month vs Yearly: Which Lens Schedule for You?

Daily disposable, monthly, 3-month, or yearly. Picking the right contact lens replacement schedule can save you 10x or cost you 5x more than necessary. Here's the math.

Contact lenses in Pakistan come in four main replacement schedules: daily disposable, monthly, 3-month (colored), and yearly (Korean). Picking the wrong schedule can cost you 5-10x more per year than necessary. Or sacrifice comfort and safety. Here's the honest math plus picks for each schedule.

Daily Disposable

Daily disposable lenses (also called "1-day" or "one-day") are worn once for 8-12 hours, then discarded at the end of the day. Fresh sterile pair every wear. Zero cleaning, no lens case needed, no solution required.

Pros:

  • Lowest infection risk of any contact lens type
  • Maximum convenience. No daily cleaning routine
  • No need to carry solution bottles when traveling
  • Best for sensitive eyes or dry-eye sufferers
  • Easiest for beginners learning lens insertion

Cons:

  • Highest per-day cost
  • Higher environmental waste
  • Limited shade range in colored daily disposables

Monthly Disposable

Monthly disposable lenses are worn for 30 days from first opening, then discarded and replaced. Most popular schedule globally. Best balance of cost and convenience for daily wearers.

Pros:

  • Best cost-per-day for daily wearers
  • Wide brand and option selection (Acuvue, Biomedics, B&L, etc.)
  • Best variety in transparent prescription lenses
  • Most silicone hydrogel options are monthly

Cons:

  • Requires daily cleaning routine
  • Need to track 30-day timer (don't exceed)
  • Higher infection risk than daily disposable
  • Need lens case (replace every 3 months)

Yearly Korean Lenses

Yearly Korean lenses (typically Glimpse, FreshKon) last 12 months with daily cleaning and proper case storage. Highest upfront cost but lowest cost per day if used regularly.

Pros:

  • Premium Korean pigment patterns and realism
  • Yearly inventory means fewer reorders

Schedule Recommendations by Wearing Pattern

If you wear lenses 1-2 days per week

→ Daily disposables. The per-day cost looks high (PKR 85+) but you only wear them 8-9 days per month, so annual cost is ~PKR 10,000. Plus zero cleaning hassle for occasional use.

If you wear lenses 3-5 days per week

→ Monthly disposable for transparent prescription (Biomedics 55, Acuvue 2). For colored, consider 3-month Bella Elite for the lowest cost.

If you wear lenses daily for 6-7 days per week

→ For colored: 3-month Bella Elite or yearly Glimpse for lowest annual cost. For transparent prescription: monthly Acuvue 2 or monthly silicone hydrogel Bausch & Lomb Ultra for AC office workers.

If you have sensitive eyes / dry eyes

→ Daily disposables regardless of frequency. Lowest infection risk justifies higher cost.

If you're a first-time lens wearer

→ Start with daily disposables (Bella One Day or Focus Dailies) for 2-3 months while learning. Switch to monthly disposables once comfortable with insertion/removal.

If you're getting married soon

→ Daily disposables for wedding events themselves (no contamination risk between events). After the wedding, switch to whatever schedule suits your post-wedding wearing pattern.

Hidden Costs Most Buyers Miss

  • Multi-purpose solution: PKR 600-800/month for reusable lens wearers
  • Lens case: PKR 200-400 every 3 months (essential for hygiene)
  • Eye drops: PKR 200-400/month for dry-eye relief
  • Optometrist visits: PKR 500-2,000 per visit, recommended annually
  • Backup pair: Most regular wearers lose/damage 1-2 pairs per year

Frequently Asked Questions

Which contact lens replacement schedule is most cost-effective?
For year-round daily wear, yearly Korean lenses (Glimpse, ~PKR 12,000 annually for both eyes) are cheapest absolute cost. For colored daily wear, 3-month Bella Elite (~PKR 22,000 annually). For transparent prescription daily wear, monthly Biomedics 55 (~PKR 42,000 annually). For occasional wear (1-3 days per week), daily disposables (Bella One Day, ~PKR 10,000-25,000 annually) are most cost-effective despite higher per-day price.
How long do colored contact lenses last?
Daily disposable colored lenses (Bella One Day): single-use, 8-12 hours then discard. Monthly colored (Freshlook ColorBlends): 30 days from first opening with daily cleaning. 3-month colored (Bella Elite, Diamond, Glow, Highlight): 90 days from first opening. Yearly Korean colored (Glimpse): 12 months with strict daily cleaning and proper case storage. Always discard at or before the scheduled replacement date.
Can I wear monthly lenses for longer than 30 days?
No. Never exceed the manufacturer's replacement schedule. Monthly lenses are designed for 30 days from first opening, regardless of how many days you actually wore them. Even if you only wore them 10 times, discard after 30 days. The lens material degrades and protein/biofilm builds up over time even with daily cleaning. Wearing past the replacement date significantly increases eye infection risk.
Should I use daily or monthly contact lenses?
Daily disposables suit occasional wearers (1-3 days/week), dry-eye sufferers, travelers, beginners, and brides needing fresh lenses for wedding events. Monthly disposables suit daily wearers (4+ days/week) who want best cost-per-day balance and don't mind daily cleaning. For weak eyesight transparent prescription, monthly is the most common choice. For colored lenses, monthly Freshlook ColorBlends or 3-month Bella Elite are popular.
