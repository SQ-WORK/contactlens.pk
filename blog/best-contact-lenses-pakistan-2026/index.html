Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best contact lens brand in Pakistan?
The best contact lens brand depends on your use case. For colored lenses, Bella is Pakistan's most popular brand (consistent quality, widest shade range, PKR 5,400 starting). For premium colored, Dahab Korean lenses (PKR 3,150). For transparent prescription, Acuvue 2 (PKR 11,950) or budget Biomedics 55 (PKR 3,450). For dry eyes / long wear, silicone hydrogel like Bausch & Lomb Ultra.
Which contact lens brand is most affordable in Pakistan?
The most affordable trusted brand is Sunsoft transparent monthly lenses at PKR 1,700, followed by Hydro and Adore colored lenses at PKR 1,900. For mid-range value, Biomedics 55 (PKR 3,450) and Eye Soft (PKR 3,650) offer the best quality-to-price ratio.
Are Bella lenses original?
Original Bella lenses arrive in factory-sealed blister packaging with Bella-branded outer box, holographic authenticity sticker, batch number on foil, and the original color name printed correctly. Counterfeits from informal markets often skip these elements. ContactLens.pk sources only through authorized Bella distribution.
