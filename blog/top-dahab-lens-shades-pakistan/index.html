Top 10 Dahab Lens Shades for Pakistani Skin Tones

Dahab makes 30+ shades across Gold, Platinum, and One Day collections. After processing hundreds of Dahab orders for Pakistani buyers, these 10 shades come up over and over again.

Dahab is one of Pakistan's most-searched colored lens brands. Korean-manufactured, Kuwait-designed, and famous for warm tones that flatter Pakistani skin. The brand has 30+ shades across three collections (Gold, Platinum, One Day), which is honestly too many for first-time buyers to navigate. Here are the 10 shades we get asked about most often, sorted by use case.

Looking for a specific shade not listed below? WhatsApp +92 321 9455233 and we'll confirm if it's in stock. Dahab releases new shades seasonally.

1. Dahab Sun Kiss. Grey-Brown Hazel Blend (All-Round Bestseller)

Sun Kiss is consistently the most-ordered Dahab shade in Pakistan. Grey-brown blend with warm hazel undertones. Works for both day and evening wear, photographs beautifully in any lighting, and suits every Pakistani skin tone from fair to deep.

Best for: brides who want one shade that works across mehndi, valima, and reception. Office wear that doesn't scream "colored lens". Casual daily wear.

2. Dahab Solitaire. Light Grey-Honey Blend

Solitaire gives a soft Western European eye look on Pakistani brown irises. Light grey-honey blend with subtle limbal ring. Very popular for daytime weddings and engagement photos.

Best for: fair-to-wheatish skin tones, daytime events.

3. Dahab Hind. Natural White-Grey

Hind is Dahab's most natural-looking shade. Pure white-grey with no limbal ring. The absence of a dark ring around the iris edge makes the lens disappear into the natural eye, creating a believable grey eye color instead of an obviously "contacted" look.

Best for: office wearers who want subtle enhancement. Brides who want grey eyes that look real in close-up wedding photos.

4. Dahab Tiffany Blue. Natural Sky Blue

Named after the Tiffany jewelry box blue, this shade is the most photogenic Dahab blue. Natural sky-blue with no limbal ring. Creates believable blue eyes without the artificial flat look of cheaper blue lenses.

Best for: photoshoots, summer events, beach weddings.

5. Dahab Swarovski (Platinum Collection)

The premium pick from the Platinum collection. Crystal pixel-pattern grey-silver. Looks stunning in flash photography and Instagram close-ups. PKR 3,500 (PKR 350 premium over Gold collection shades).

Best for: Instagram-focused brides, fashion photography, content creators.

6. Dahab Lumiere Series (Hazel, Gray, Blue, Green, Brown)

The Lumiere series is Dahab's most versatile sub-collection. Five shades in the same pixel pattern but different color tones. Lumiere Hazel is the bestseller (warm hazel-honey blend, suits Pakistani skin well), followed by Lumiere Gray and Lumiere Blue.

Best for: buyers who want to try multiple Dahab shades in the same finish. Couples buying coordinated lens looks.

7. Dahab Sabrin Gray Green. Unique Two-Tone

Sabrin Gray Green is unlike any other shade. A gray-green dual tone that shifts in different lighting. Most-asked-about shade by university students looking for something different.

Best for: daily wear with personality, unique social media aesthetic.

8. Dahab Caramel. Light Green-Brown

Caramel is the global bestseller from Dahab Gold. Light green-brown blend that gives natural enhancement without dramatic transformation. Particularly popular among Pakistani buyers in Saudi Arabia and the UAE.

Best for: daily office wear, natural-look fans.

9. Dahab Sabrin Soul. Soft Grey for Daily

Soft grey shade with subtle limbal ring. Bridges the gap between Hind (no ring) and Solitaire (defined ring). Most balanced grey shade in the Dahab range.

10. Dahab Sky / Aqua. Cool-Tone Variants

For buyers wanting blue tones lighter than Tiffany Blue, Sky and Aqua are softer cool-blue variants. Sky has more grey, Aqua leans turquoise.
