Contact Lens for Astigmatism in Pakistan: Toric Lens Guide

If your prescription has a CYL value, you have astigmatism and need toric contact lenses. Not regular spherical lenses. Here's the complete Pakistani buyer's guide.

If you've been told you have astigmatism, your eye prescription will include a non-zero CYL value. Standard spherical contact lenses won't give you sharp vision. You need toric lenses, which are specifically weighted to correct astigmatism.

This guide covers everything Pakistani astigmatism buyers need to know: how to read your prescription for toric ordering, which brands are available, current prices, and how to verify the right lens for your eyes.

What Is Astigmatism?

Astigmatism is an irregular cornea shape. Instead of being perfectly spherical (like a basketball), the cornea is more oval (like a rugby ball). This causes light to focus at multiple points on the retina, creating blurry vision at all distances. Around 30-40% of people have some degree of astigmatism, often combined with myopia (nearsightedness).

You can tell if you have astigmatism by checking your eye prescription card. If you see a CYL value other than zero (like -0.75, -1.25, -2.00), you have astigmatism. Standard contact lenses won't correct it.

How Toric Lenses Work

Toric lenses correct astigmatism in three ways:

  1. Different powers across the lens. Standard lenses have one power across the whole lens. Toric lenses have different powers in different lens zones to match the irregular cornea.
  2. Weighted to stay oriented. Toric lenses have a thicker bottom edge (or ballast design) that uses gravity to keep the lens in the correct rotational position on your eye.
  3. SPH + CYL + AXIS combination. Three values must match your eye for the lens to work. Sphere power, cylinder power, and the axis direction.

How to Read Your Astigmatism Prescription

Your eye prescription card from the optometrist will have these values:

ValueWhat It MeansExample
SPH (Sphere)Main power. Same as standard prescription-2.50
CYL (Cylinder)Astigmatism power. Needed for toric lens-1.25
AXISDirection of astigmatism (degrees 1-180)180
BCBase curve (lens fit)8.6
DIALens diameter14.5

Example: -2.50 SPH / -1.25 CYL / AXIS 180 means you need a toric lens with -2.50 sphere power, -1.25 cylinder power, axis 180 degrees.

You need both eyes' prescription separately: left and right eyes often have different SPH, CYL, and AXIS values.

Toric Lens First-Time Tips

  • Expect adjustment. Toric lenses take 1-2 weeks longer to adjust to than spherical lenses because of the weighted design. Vision may shift slightly when you blink as the lens settles.
  • Insert with the marker at the bottom. Most toric lenses have a small marker showing correct orientation. Insert with the marker facing 6 o'clock.
  • Don't worry about minor rotation. The lens self-orients within a few seconds of insertion. Some movement during blinking is normal.
  • Get refitted if vision feels wrong. If after 2 weeks your vision is still blurry, return to your optometrist. The lens may need a different base curve or you may need a different toric brand.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between toric and regular contact lenses?
Regular (spherical) contact lenses have the same power across the entire lens. They correct myopia (-) and hyperopia (+). Toric lenses have different powers in different lens zones to match an irregular astigmatic cornea, plus they're weighted to stay rotationally stable. Toric lenses require three prescription values (SPH + CYL + AXIS) instead of just sphere power.
How do I know if I need toric contact lenses?
Check your eye prescription card for a CYL value. If CYL is zero or absent, you have spherical vision and can use regular contact lenses. If CYL has any value other than zero (like -0.75, -1.25, -2.00), you have astigmatism and need toric lenses. Around 30-40% of people have some degree of astigmatism.
What is the price of toric contact lenses in Pakistan?

Can I get colored contact lenses if I have astigmatism?
Yes, but options are limited. We stock Dahab Toric Eyesight as our main colored+toric option. Most colored lens brands (Bella, Freshlook, regular Dahab Gold, Glimpse) do not make toric versions. Common workaround: clear toric Biomedics Toric for everyday wear plus single-use Bella One Day colored daily disposables for events. This avoids special-ordering colored toric and keeps both affordable.
How long does it take toric lenses to settle on my eyes?
Toric lenses typically take 1-2 weeks longer to adjust to than regular spherical lenses because of the weighted ballast design. During the first few days, you may notice slight vision shifts when you blink as the lens settles into correct rotational position. By the end of week 2, the lens should feel comparable to spherical lenses. If vision is still blurry after 2 weeks, consult your optometrist.
