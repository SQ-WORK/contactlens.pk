Best Hazel Contact Lenses in Pakistan: 8 Top Picks for Brown Eyes

Hazel is the most-searched colored lens shade in Pakistan, and the most flattering on brown eyes. Here are the 8 best hazel lenses ranked by what they're best for.

Hazel is the bestseller. Across our customer base in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, and 500+ Pakistani cities, hazel lenses outsell every other shade combined. There are three reasons: hazel blends with the natural amber-gold flecks already in Pakistani brown eyes, it works for both day and evening settings, and it photographs beautifully on every skin tone from fair to deep.

But "hazel" covers a wide spectrum. From cool brown-green to warm honey-amber. Here are the 8 hazel shades we'd actually recommend, ranked by what they're best for.

1. Freshlook ColorBlends Pure Hazel. The Bestseller

The single most-prescribed hazel lens in Pakistan. Cool brown-green undertones with Freshlook's 3-in-1 color blend technology. Yellow + brown + black layered to mimic natural iris depth. Monthly disposable, soft hydrogel material (highest in the hazel category), PKR 5,850.

Best for: first-time hazel buyers, dry-eye sufferers, AC office workers.

Best for: events, weddings, daytime social occasions, warm skin tones.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which hazel contact lens is most natural-looking on Pakistani eyes?
For the most natural hazel look, Freshlook ColorBlends Pure Hazel is the top pick. Its 3-in-1 color blend technology (yellow + brown + black layered) mimics real iris depth. Bella Elite Wild Honey is also excellent for slightly warmer golden undertones. For premium realism, Glimpse Hazelnut Korean yearly lenses have the most realistic pixel patterns.
What is the price of hazel contact lenses in Pakistan?
Hazel contact lens prices in Pakistan range from PKR 2,650 for Bella One Day Hazel Honey daily disposables to PKR 6,500+ for premium Glimpse Hazelnut yearly lenses. The most popular Freshlook Pure Hazel is PKR 5,850 and Bella Elite Wild Honey is PKR 5,400 for 3-month wear.
Are hazel contact lenses good for dark brown eyes?
Yes. Hazel is the most-flattering colored lens shade for dark Pakistani brown eyes. Hazel pigment blends with your natural amber-gold iris flecks instead of completely covering them, so the result looks like a brighter version of your real eyes. For complete transformation choose high-opacity Bella Elite Wild Honey; for subtle daily wear, Freshlook Pure Hazel.
Can I get hazel contact lenses with eyesight power?
Yes. Most hazel lenses are available in prescription powers from -0.50 to -8.00 Sph in 0.25 increments. Freshlook ColorBlends Pure Hazel, Bella Elite Wild Honey, and Glimpse Hazelnut all support prescription orders. WhatsApp +92 321 9455233 to confirm power availability for your specific shade.
