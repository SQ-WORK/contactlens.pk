  • Bella Elite Midnight Blue: deep dramatic navy-blue, high opacity for dark eyes. 3 months wear. PKR 5,400.
  • Glimpse Sapphire: premium Korean yearly lens with crystal-pattern sapphire blue.
  • Glimpse Maya Blue: soft Mayan turquoise-blue, suits both day and event wear.
  • Freshlook True Sapphire: vibrant sapphire blue with three-tone blend.
  • Dahab Tiffany Blue (coming soon). Korean natural sky-blue with no limbal ring.

Choosing the Right Blue Shade for Pakistani Eyes

Dark brown Pakistani eyes need high pigment opacity for blue lenses to look believable rather than dull. The lens type matters:

  • For maximum drama: Bella Elite Midnight Blue covers dark eyes completely with deep navy pigment. Best for events, mehndi, photoshoots.
  • For natural everyday wear: Freshlook ColorBlends Blue uses 3-in-1 technology to blend blue with your natural iris pigment, creating depth rather than flat color.
  • For light Pakistani eyes (light brown / hazel-flecked). Softer shades like Glimpse Maya Blue or Tiffany Blue look stunning without going too dramatic.

How Long Do Blue Contact Lenses Last?

It depends on lens type:

  • Daily disposable blue lenses: wear once for 8-12 hours, throw away. Ideal for weddings or occasional bold looks.
  • Monthly blue lenses (Freshlook ColorBlends Blue). 30 days from first opening with daily cleaning.
  • 3-month blue lenses (Bella Elite Midnight Blue). 90 days with proper care.
  • Yearly blue lenses (Glimpse Sapphire). 12 months with daily cleaning and case storage.
