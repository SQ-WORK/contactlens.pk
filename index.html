Daily, Monthly, or 3-Month. Which Lens Schedule Suits You?

Contact lenses come in different replacement schedules. Choose based on how often you'll wear them:

  • Daily disposables (Bella One Day, Freshkon One-Day, Focus Dailies). Wear once, throw away. Zero cleaning. Best for occasional wear or weddings.
  • Monthly disposables (Acuvue 2, Biomedics 55, Eye Soft, Bausch & Lomb Soflens). Last 30 days with daily cleaning. Best balance of cost and convenience.
  • 3-month colored lenses (Bella Elite, Bella Diamond, Glow, Highlight). Last 90 days. Lowest cost per wear for colored lens regulars.
  • Yearly Korean lenses (Glimpse Swarovski, Hazelnut, Cappucino). Last 12 months with proper care. Lowest absolute cost over time.

Are contact lenses safe to wear daily?
Yes, contact lenses from authorized brands (Bella, Dahab, Freshlook, Acuvue, Bausch & Lomb) are safe for daily wear when used correctly. Follow the recommended replacement schedule, clean reusable lenses with fresh multi-purpose solution after each wear, never sleep in lenses unless specifically labeled extended wear, and never use water on lenses. Replace your lens case every 3 months for hygiene.
