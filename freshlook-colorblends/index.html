Why FreshLook Outperforms Other Colored Lenses

1. 3-in-1 Color Blend Technology

Each FreshLook ColorBlends lens combines three colors in a precise pattern to recreate natural iris depth. Other colored lenses use a single solid color which can look flat or unnatural up close. FreshLook layers, for example: yellow + brown + black for the Pure Hazel shade, recreating the natural way light reflects off a real iris.

2. Higher soft hydrogel water content)

FreshLook ColorBlends has soft hydrogel material. Significantly higher than Bella (38%), Dahab (38%), or Glimpse. This makes FreshLook the most comfortable choice for:

  • Air-conditioned offices in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad
  • Long daily wear (10-12 hours)
  • Dry-eye sufferers
  • First-time colored lens wearers

3. UV Blocking

FreshLook ColorBlends blocks Class 2 UV. Adding protection on top of the cosmetic effect. Useful for outdoor weddings and Pakistan's intense summer sun.

4. Made by Alcon. World's #1 Eye Care Company

Alcon (subsidiary of Novartis) is the largest eye care company globally, with FDA, CE, and TGA certifications. FreshLook lenses are manufactured in the USA and Ireland.
