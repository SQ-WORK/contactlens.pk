Bella Elite Collection. The Bestseller

The Elite collection is Bella's flagship line and the most-searched contact lens in Pakistan. Elite features 12+ vibrant shades with high-opacity pigment that fully covers dark Pakistani brown eyes. Popular shades include Emerald Green, Amber Grey, Cloudy Grey, Midnight Blue, Mint Grey, Lavender Grey, Crystal N, Sandy Brown, Sandy Grey, Silky Green, Wild Honey, and Grey Beige. Each pair lasts 90 days with proper care and is available in plano (0.00) plus prescription powers up to -8.00 Sph.

Bella Diamond Collection

Diamond uses Bella's three-tone pigment technology. A darker outer ring, intermediate band, and lighter center. To mimic the natural iris depth. The result is the most realistic colored lens in Bella's range. Diamond runs PKR 4,950 with similar 3-month replacement schedule. Shades include Diamond Agate, Diamond Mysterious, Diamond Silver Mist, Diamond Oak.

Bella Highlight Collection

Highlight is for buyers who want the trendy dolly-eye look. A defined dark limbal ring around the iris edge makes eyes appear larger and more striking. Popular among younger Pakistani buyers and university students. Cool Hazel, Circle Gray, and Pearl Gray are top sellers from this collection.

Bella One Day. Daily Disposables

Bella One Day is Pakistan's most popular daily disposable colored lens. Wear once, throw away. No solution, no case, no cleaning. The lowest infection risk of any colored lens option. Available in 10 shades including Almond Brown, Hazel Honey, Platinum Grey, Ash Brown, Rose Wood, Cedar, Pine. PKR 2,650 per pair (for a single day's wear), or buy a bundle of 5 pairs.

Bella for Weak Eyesight

All Bella collections (except Natural, which is plano only) are available in prescription powers from -0.50 Sph up to -8.00 Sph in 0.25 increments. WhatsApp your prescription card to +92 321 9455233 before ordering. We'll confirm exact power availability and dispatch the matching pair. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price of Bella contact lenses in Pakistan?
Bella contact lens prices in Pakistan range from PKR 2,650 for Bella One Day daily disposables up to PKR 5,400 for Bella Elite 3-month wear. Mid-range Bella Diamond runs PKR 4,950, Bella Glow PKR 4,500, Bella Highlight PKR 4,250, and Bella Natural PKR 3,950. All Bella lenses ship with Rs 350 flat delivery nationwide.
Which Bella collection is best for dark Pakistani eyes?
Bella Elite has the highest pigment opacity in the range and provides the most complete transformation on dark brown Pakistani eyes. Especially Emerald Green, Midnight Blue, Amber Grey, and Cloudy Grey. For a more natural look on dark eyes, Bella Natural (semi-translucent blend) or Bella Highlight (with limbal ring) are excellent choices. Bella One Day Almond Brown and Hazel Honey work well for subtle daily wear.
Are Bella lenses available with eyesight (power)?
Yes, Bella Elite, Diamond, Glow, Highlight, and One Day collections are available in prescription powers from -0.50 to -8.00 Sph in 0.25 increments. Bella Natural is plano (no power) only. Send your prescription card via WhatsApp to +92 321 9455233 and we'll confirm power availability before dispatching.
How long do Bella contact lenses last?
Bella One Day lenses are daily disposables. Worn once for 8-12 hours and then discarded. Bella Elite, Diamond, Glow, Highlight, and Natural collections are 3-month (90-day) replacement lenses. With proper daily cleaning using multi-purpose solution and case storage, a single Bella Elite pair lasts the full 90 days from first opening.
Are Bella lenses safe?
Yes. Bella lenses are manufactured to ISO 13485 medical device standards using internationally distributed Polymacon hydrogel material with Class 1 UV-A and UV-B blocking. The soft hydrogel material and 8.6mm base curve fit most Pakistani eye shapes comfortably. Wash hands before handling, use fresh multi-purpose solution daily for reusable lenses, and never wear past the replacement schedule.
What's the difference between Bella Elite and Bella Diamond?
Bella Elite uses high-opacity single-tone pigment for maximum color coverage. Best for dramatic transformation on dark eyes. Bella Diamond uses three-tone layered pigment (dark outer ring, mid-tone band, light center) to mimic natural iris depth. Best for a realistic close-up look. Elite is more dramatic, Diamond is more believable. Price difference is PKR 450.
