Why Gold Lenses Work for Pakistani Weddings

Gold contact lenses pair perfectly with traditional Pakistani bridal looks. They match gold jewelry, gold-thread bridal dresses, and complement henna mehndi designs. Three reasons gold is a Pakistani wedding staple:

  • Complements gold jewelry: matching tones create cohesive bridal photography
  • Glows in candlelight: warm tones reflect golden indoor lighting beautifully
  • Subtle enough for the full bridal day: won't compete with makeup and jewelry attention

Gold Lens Care

Dahab Gold lenses come with complimentary lens solution sachet and a Dahab-branded lens case in original packaging. Replace solution daily, never top up old solution, and store lenses in fresh solution overnight.
